06 / ABOUT

LAND COVER,

THE EUROSAT BENCHMARK

EuroSAT is a benchmark dataset based on Sentinel-2 satellite imagery covering 13 spectral bands spanning 10 land-use and land-cover categories across Europe. The categories reflect critical geographic, urban, and agricultural interfaces: Annual Crop, Forest, Herbaceous Vegetation, Highway, Industrial, Pasture, Permanent Crop, Residential, River, and Sea/Lake.

WHY 13 SPECTRAL BANDS MATTER

Standard optical RGB channels (Red, Green, Blue) miss key physical properties of the Earth's surface. Sentinel-2's Vegetation Red Edge bands (B05–B07) reveal chlorophyll concentration, while the Near Infrared (B08, B08A) and Shortwave Infrared (B11, B12) bands penetrate atmospheric moisture and discriminate between soil types, irrigation levels, and industrial materials.

INFERENCE INTEGRITY

Every prediction rendered in this application is calculated live by the PyTorch runtime. Digital Numbers are scaled to physical surface reflectance by dividing by 10,000, normalized against channel statistics, and fed through the active deep neural checkpoint.
